Free, private photo viewer that shows **where a photo was taken**. It reads GPS from EXIF and puts the spot on Google Maps, Yandex Maps and OpenStreetMap side by side, with the street address, time, camera, photos of the area and nearby Wikipedia articles.

![Screenshots](docs/screenshots/theme-brand.png)

## Features

- **Your photos stay with you.** They are never uploaded and are read right on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
- **Where it was taken, on the map.** Google, Yandex, OpenStreetMap and satellite in one window.
- **Address** and coordinates of every photo, plus links to street panoramas.
- **Photos of the area by other people** and a Wikipedia guide to what is nearby.
- **All photos on one map** and the route of your trip.
- **Time and camera** of the shot next to the coordinates.
- **Place search** for photos without coordinates.
- **Installs as an app** and works offline.

## Privacy

Photos are opened and read in your browser. They are never uploaded. Only the coordinates of a point are sent to map, address and Wikipedia services. No account, no ads, no analytics.

## Install

- Online: open https://ildargiv.github.io/photogeofinder/ in Chrome, Edge or Yandex Browser and click "Install app". Updates arrive automatically.
- Offline copy: download the latest zip from [Releases](https://github.com/ildargiv/photogeofinder/releases/latest), unzip and run `start-windows.bat` (Windows, needs Python) or `start-mac-linux.command`. The app tells you when a new release is out. You can also just open `index.html`.
